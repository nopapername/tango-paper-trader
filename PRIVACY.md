# Privacy Policy

Tango Paper Trader stores settings, paper balances, positions, and simulated trade history locally in the browser using Chrome extension storage.

The extension does not request wallet access, private keys, seed phrases, email addresses, or payment information. It does not submit blockchain transactions. Core paper-trading data is not uploaded to Tango or sold to third parties.

The extension reads public token information displayed on supported GMGN pages and locally reuses public token quote fields from responses GMGN has already requested. It requests public native-asset/USD reference prices for registered chains from CoinGecko. When GMGN and the visible page do not provide enough quote data, it may request a public token quote from DEX Screener only for chains with an explicit registry mapping.

Public price and token-quote requests can contain a registered chain identifier or public token contract address. They do not contain wallet addresses, wallet data, private keys, paper balances, positions, trade history, or other personal information.

Users can export, import, or permanently reset their local data from the dashboard.
